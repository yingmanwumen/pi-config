# Research and Documentation

Use this workflow for briefs, summaries, comparisons, investigations, or reports based on multiple Notion sources.

1. Define the question, audience, scope, output format, and date boundary.
2. Search with targeted literal queries, refine separately, and confirm the source set when results are ambiguous.
3. Fetch each relevant page and recursively read its blocks. Track source IDs, URLs, dates, claims, metrics, constraints, and contradictions.
4. Choose a quick brief, research summary, comparison, or comprehensive report.
5. Synthesize by theme or question, preserving uncertainty and distinguishing evidence from inference.
6. Create the output under an explicit parent with inline source links and a references section.
7. Re-fetch the result and report gaps, conflicting sources, recommendations, and the resulting URL.

Do not cite search results that are external connected-source URLs as if they were Notion pages. Only fetch and cite pages or databases accessible through the Notion API.
